# The Brief — Fictional Newsroom

The byline is part of the joke, not decoration. Assign every article to a writer whose established worldview naturally creates the comic angle.

## Assignment rules

- Reuse an existing writer when the story advances that writer's beat, fixation or feud.
- Introduce a new correspondent only when the story needs a perspective the current masthead cannot supply.
- A new writer needs a beat, background, reporting method, page voice and disclosed tendency before receiving a byline.
- Inspirations may come from recognizable modes of journalism, broadcasting, comedy or fiction, but the resulting character must be original rather than an imitation or renamed real person.
- Do not make every writer hostile to the same managers. Rotate attention and allow some writers to become strangely protective of a subject.
- Their biases should influence framing and language, never alter the underlying facts.
- Let personalities deepen through accumulated coverage. Avoid restating the entire biography inside an article.
