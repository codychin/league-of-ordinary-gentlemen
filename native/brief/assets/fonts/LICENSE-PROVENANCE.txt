THE BRIEF SEVEN — PROJECT PROVENANCE / PROTOTYPE USE NOTE

Created for the user's The Brief project on 2026-10-31 from original, newly authored Bézier letterforms. The direction-07 drawings created in this conversation are the starting material; new uppercase, lowercase, numeral, punctuation and accent drawings extend those originals.
